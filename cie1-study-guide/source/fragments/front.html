Start here: how to use this guide

This guide covers exactly the CIE-1 portion of Software Engineering & Modelling (CS51): all of Unit 1, all of Unit 2, and Unit 3 up to architectural views. It assumes you know nothing yet. Every point from your lecture slides is here, explained in plain English first, then in the textbook wording you should use in answers.

How each chapter works

  1. Opener page: what the chapter covers, how long it takes and what you will be able to do.
  2. Sections: each idea is explained simply, then given in exam wording, with an example and a diagram where one helps.
  3. Chapter in one page: a dense revision sheet. Re-read it the night before the exam.
  4. Likely exam questions: each one says what to write and which diagram to draw.

Priority badges

Must know

Asked often: definitions, models, diagrams, comparisons. Study these first and practise the diagrams.

Important

Commonly asked as short questions or parts of long answers. Understand them well.

Read once

Background or lower-priority material. Read it once so nothing surprises you.

Study plans

If you have a week

  • Day 1: Ch 1 + start Ch 2
  • Day 2: finish Ch 2 and draw every model
  • Day 3: Ch 3
  • Day 4: Ch 4
  • Day 5: Ch 5 + Ch 6
  • Day 6: all question banks
  • Day 7: one-page recaps + diagrams

If you have 3 days

  • Day 1: Ch 1–3, focusing on Must know
  • Day 2: Ch 4–6, focusing on Must know
  • Day 3: question banks, then recaps; redraw every diagram once

If it's the night before

  • Read all six one-page recaps
  • Redraw the must-know diagrams from memory
  • Skim each question bank and say the answer outline out loud
  • Sleep; recall is better when you're rested

For anything worth more than 2 marks: definition → diagram → explanation in points → example → advantages/disadvantages (or comparison). A neat, labelled diagram earns marks even when your text is short.
