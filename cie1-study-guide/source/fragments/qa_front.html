Read this first

This booklet gives you the 5-mark questions most likely to appear in CIE-1, 7–8 per unit, each with a model answer written the way you should write it in about 10 minutes: definition, a diagram you can draw quickly, and numbered points with keywords. Each unit ends with backup questions, and the last section is a predicted CIE-1 paper.

What last year's paper tells us

Unit 3 is guaranteed

Q1 is compulsory and always has a CO3 part. Unit 3's CIE-1 portion is small (7 questions here), so it is the easiest guaranteed 5 marks.

Units 1 & 2 carry the rest

In 2024: 4 parts from Unit 1, 4 from Unit 2, 1 from Unit 3. Prepare Units 1 and 2 well enough to have a free choice between Q2 and Q3.

Diagrams score

Every part was "explain/describe" (Bloom L1–L2), and 3 of 9 demanded a neat labelled diagram (RE spiral, Scrum sprint cycle, incremental model).

The 10-minute answer method

  1. 1 minute: plan. Write the definition and list your point headings in the margin.
  2. 3 minutes: draw. Draw the diagram first, neat and labelled, with a caption.
  3. 5 minutes: write. Numbered points with a bold or underlined keyword and one explaining line each.
  4. 1 minute: check. Did you answer every part of the question (for example "advantages and problems")?
